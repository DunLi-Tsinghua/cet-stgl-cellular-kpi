\subsection{Limitations, Data Availability and Reproducibility}
\label{sec:limitations_reproducibility}
\label{sec:discussion_limitations}
\label{sec:data_availability}

The findings are bounded by the evaluated panel and weak-label protocol. KPI histories and temporal-cell context provide the main forecasting signal, while alarm and inferred graph features show limited and non-uniform aggregate gains. Weak alarm-candidate ranking is a proxy consistency check rather than expert-confirmed RCA, and the event influence graph reflects observational associations rather than causal-effect estimates.

The chronological split evaluates future-time generalization for observed cell entities, not unseen regions, networks or deployments. End-to-end neural spatio-temporal graph evaluation remains future work. Raw Huawei MBB records cannot be publicly released because they contain confidential operational KPI and alarm information; source code, sanitized schemas, synthetic validation data and experiment scripts are provided at \url{https://github.com/DunLi-Tsinghua/cet-stgl-cellular-kpi}.
